Project brief

⟳ Regenerate

What this is. CLT-DevSpec replaces the manual dispatch operation that CLT runs out of its Vietnam depots today. One ops planner builds every route by hand in Excel at 06:00 and emails the sheet to the depot; the programme's purpose is to make that planning system-driven and to put a proof of delivery in the driver's hand instead of on paper.

Who it is for. The daily users are depot dispatchers and ~400 drivers. Operations (Ms. Hanh) owns the dispatch rules and signs them off; IT security reviews anything that leaves the building; a finance approver is referenced throughout the SRS but never named.

What is being built. Phase 1 is order capture → dispatch planning → proof of delivery, Vietnam region only, with a mobile driver app. Billing stays on the legacy SAP instance — replacing it is explicitly excluded — and there is no web driver portal. Invoicing is referred to as a phase-2 follow-on but was never committed to.

What constrains it. Go-live is contractual rather than a target, and the two documents disagree on the date. All customer data must stay inside the region, with no cross-border replication. Volume is quoted at ~12,000 orders a day with no response-time or concurrency target anywhere in the material.

What is still unknown. No external system is described anywhere, so every integration is an open question — including the telematics feed the kickoff assumes. Returns and failed deliveries are mentioned once with no process. Ownership of customer master data is never settled.
